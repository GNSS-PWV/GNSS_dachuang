# Causal phase-one retraining

`train_causal_phase1.py` is an intermediate retraining entry point. It uses
`causal_features.py`, fits both scalers on the train split only, and applies
the requested 10% station holdout with seed 42 before the year split.

The SLURM launcher defaults to a one-epoch, ten-file smoke run. The full mode
trains PS for 30 epochs. WPS, TS, and Tm should be submitted as separate jobs
after the PS smoke artifacts pass. Results are phase-one forecasting results,
not independent end-to-end phase-two GNSS-PWV accuracy.
