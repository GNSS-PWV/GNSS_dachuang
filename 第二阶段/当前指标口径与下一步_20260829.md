# Current metric status and next step (2026-08-29)

## Verified status

The historical phase-two `0.05--0.4 mm` results use radiosonde-derived true
ZWD and reconstructed-profile labels. They are valid as a pipeline/model
consistency check, but they are not an independent end-to-end GNSS deployment
metric. The existing local 2019 GNSS files provide a more operational
reference for GPT3; the pooled raw GPT3 result is about `1.70 mm` across nine
stations and 761,184 records.

## Required before a scientific retraining claim

1. Adapt all phase-one training launchers to the causal feature contract and
   fit scalers on the training split only.
2. Build phase-two inputs from independent GNSS `ZTD - ZHD` and non-target
   profiles (ERA5, climatology, or phase-one predictions).
3. Use independent PWV references from radiosonde/EC/GNSS with documented
   provenance and the agreed split: 2014--2016 train, 2017 test, 2018
   validation, 10% leave-station validation (seed 42), and 2019 final
   validation.
4. Retrain on the Tongji GPU/SLURM environment after the input chain is
   complete.

Until these conditions are satisfied, report old scores as `oracle/closed-loop
pipeline validation`, not as final GNSS-PWV accuracy.
